Trockel, Ulmann & Freunde & Friends

A small café tucked into Pembroke Street, run the way cafés used to be run — handmade panini, soup of the day, German-style cakes, and properly good coffee. The clue's in the name: und Freunde — & Friends. Most of ours have been coming for years.

Im Café

A café, not a chain.

Trockel, Ulmann & Freunde has sat on Pembroke Street for many years, in the shadow of Pembroke College — a small room with window stools, shelves of mismatched things, and the smell of something warming on the hob.

It's the kind of place where the people behind the counter remember your order and where the menu shifts with what's been baked that morning. The German half of the name is honest — cakes are baked the way you'd find them in a Konditorei, the soups change with the week, and the coffee is taken seriously without ceremony.

Walk-in only. Sit by the window with a panini and watch Pembroke Street go by. Or grab a takeaway cup and a slice of something warm on your way to a lecture. That's the whole offer.
